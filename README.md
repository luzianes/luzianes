### Main projects

**[PreviDengue](https://github.com/luzianes/PreviDengue)** — Dengue risk forecasting for Recife by neighborhood and health district, from SINAN case data (2016–2025) and ERA5 climate data. Direct and chained forecasting strategies, experiments tracked in MLflow, served by a FastAPI service and an interactive risk map with alerts and trend analysis.
`Python` `scikit-learn` `MLflow` `FastAPI` `Dash` `Docker`

**[lh-nautical-desafio](https://github.com/luzianes/lh-nautical-desafio)** — End-to-end data pipeline over 24 operational CSVs (2020–2026): schema generation from raw files, load into PostgreSQL, analytical SQL, demand forecasting, product recommendation and a self-contained dashboard.
`Python` `PostgreSQL` `SQL` `Power BI` `Docker`

**[E14-LED](https://github.com/luzianes/e14-led)** — ELT pipeline for NYC Yellow Taxi trip records: raw layer, profiling, cleaning, star schema and analytical queries, fully idempotent.
`DuckDB` `SQL` `Parquet`

**[projeto-cyber](https://github.com/luzianes/projeto-cyber)** *(in progress)* — SecureAI Lab: attacking and hardening a working web application (Aponte Cafés, a Django app for finding coffee shops in Recife). Full cycle of threat modeling and risk matrix → controlled exploitation → fix → retest, covering authentication with privilege levels, API and database security, symmetric and asymmetric cryptography, SHA-256 integrity checks, data anonymization, and the risks of the application's AI feature.
`Python` `Django` `PostgreSQL` `JavaScript`
